Track My Groceries Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026
App: Track My Groceries (Android package com.davidgoodman.trackmygroceries)
Developer: Starving Apex Productions
Contact: starvingapex@gmail.com

Track My Groceries helps you keep a list of the food in your pantry, see what is running low or expiring, build a grocery list, and write meal ideas from what you have. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your pantry and recipes stay on your phone, and the app has no accounts and no ads. The one exception to "nothing leaves the phone" is the Google ML Kit text reader used for recipe pictures, described below.

Information stored on your phone

Everything you enter (item names, amounts, sizes, categories, expiration dates, shelf life, low-stock alerts, your grocery list, your saved recipes, and settings) is stored only on your phone, inside the app. We do not run a server, and we never receive a copy of your pantry.

Camera and photos

  • The app asks for camera access to scan barcodes. Barcode scanning happens on your phone. Camera frames are not saved and are not sent anywhere.
  • If you choose to add a photo to an item (by taking a picture or picking one from your gallery), the app shrinks it to a small thumbnail and saves it on your phone with that item. Photos are never uploaded by the app.
  • When a product lookup returns a product picture, the app downloads that public picture and keeps a small copy on your phone.

Product lookups (barcode numbers)

When you scan or type a barcode, the app sends only that barcode number to these public product databases to fill in the name, size, and label details:

  • Open Food Facts (world.openfoodfacts.org), and
  • UPCitemdb (api.upcitemdb.com), used as a fallback for package size.

No name, email, account, location, or pantry list is sent with these lookups. Like any website, these services can see the internet address your request comes from. Their own privacy policies apply to their services.

Backups

  • Android backup. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include this app's data in your phone's backup to your own Google account, and restore it on a new phone. This is Google's backup service, it is controlled by your phone's settings, and it is governed by Google's privacy policy. We cannot see or access it.
  • Backups you make. If you choose to save or share a backup file, it goes only where you send it (for example a folder on your phone, Google Drive, or email). Shared grocery lists and meal prompts also go only where you choose.

Mom's Recipes

  • Recipes you type, paste, or import are saved only on your phone and are included in backup files you make.
  • Import from Google. If you paste a recipe web address, your phone downloads that page directly from the recipe website to read the ingredient list. Nothing else is sent with that request, and we never see it. That website's own privacy policy applies.
  • Import from a picture. If you take or pick a picture of a recipe, the text is read on your phone using Google ML Kit text recognition. The picture is not uploaded and is not saved by the app.
  • Google ML Kit diagnostics. The ML Kit library is made by Google. Google states that, even though pictures stay on the device, ML Kit sends Google limited usage and performance information: device details (such as model and Android version), app details (package name and version), a per-installation identifier, performance numbers, error codes, and the size of the input and output. It does not include your picture or recipe text. This information is encrypted in transit and is governed by Google's terms and privacy policy (https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms). We do not receive it.
  • Copy a prompt for your AI app. This only copies text for you to paste. The app does not call any AI service.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows reminders on your phone (for example, when an item is low). These are created on the phone. Nothing is sent to us.

Meal Builder

The Meal Builder only writes text from your pantry for you to copy or share. The app does not call any AI service. If you open ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, or Google from the app and paste that text, you are using that service directly, and its own privacy policy applies.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No ads.
  • No analytics or advertising tracking of our own. (Google ML Kit diagnostics are described above.)
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information.
  • We do not collect your location or contacts, and we do not receive any identifiers.

Children

Track My Groceries is a general household app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

You can delete any item or recipe in the app, or remove all data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. Backups you saved yourself, and your Android backup in your Google account, can be deleted where they are stored.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
